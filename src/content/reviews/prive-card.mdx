## Who is the Prive Card for?

Prive targets people who move larger amounts of money across borders: freelancers, founders and frequent travellers who want one wallet for stablecoins and several fiat currencies, plus a premium card to spend from it. With limits of up to US$100,000 per transaction on higher verification tiers, it is built for high-value spending rather than small everyday top-ups.

It is harder to judge on cost. Prive keeps its card fees on a separate fees page and describes its FX only as "competitive", so you need to check the numbers yourself before committing.

## How the card works

Prive is a private global wallet. You can hold stablecoins (USDC, USDT, EURC and PYUSD) in self-custodial wallets, where Prive says you keep your own keys, and you can fund in fiat: GBP, USD, EUR, NGN, KES, MXN and BRL. Withdrawals reach local bank accounts in 80+ countries, and the wallet also supports payment links and curated concierge access.

The card is the **Prive Visa Platinum**, linked to your wallet and available as a virtual or physical card. Prive says it is accepted at more than 130 million locations worldwide.

## Fees and limits

- **Limits:** up to US$100,000 per transaction. Your actual limits depend on your verification tier.
- **Card fees:** Prive publishes them on a separate card fees page, not on its main site. Check it for issuance, monthly and ATM costs before ordering.
- **FX:** described as competitive, but no rate or markup is published on the main site.

We do not show fee numbers for Prive until we can confirm them on Prive's own fee page.

## KYC and availability

Prive uses verification tiers: the more you verify, the higher your limits. It does not publish the documents each tier needs. Its privacy policy says it may support reusable KYC, so identity data verified elsewhere could be reused where available, though Prive does not guarantee this.

Prive does not publish a list of supported or restricted countries. Its fiat funding currencies and 80+ off-ramp countries suggest broad coverage, but confirm eligibility in the app before signing up.

## Verdict

Prive is a strong concept for high-value, multi-currency users: self-custodial stablecoin wallets, wide fiat funding, bank withdrawals in 80+ countries and a Visa Platinum card with limits up to US$100,000 per transaction. What holds it back is disclosure. Until you have checked the card fees page and your tier's limits, treat it as a promising option rather than a proven bargain.
