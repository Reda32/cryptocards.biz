## Who is Plasma One for?

Plasma One suits people who already hold stablecoins and want to spend them like cash — self-custodially, without selling into a bank account first. If most of your balance is USDT or USDC and you want zero-fee transfers plus cashback and yield while you spend, it is one of the most complete stablecoin cards available.

It is a poor fit if you want a simple sign-up with no invite code, if you dislike being paid rewards in a volatile token, or if you are uncomfortable with DeFi risk. The yield comes from third-party vaults and is not guaranteed.

## How the card works

You download the app, enter a free access code, verify your identity, and top up with USDT or USDC. The balance is held self-custodially — Plasma does not take custody — and you spend through a Visa card that can be added to Apple Pay or Google Pay within minutes. Transactions and transfers route over the Plasma network, which is purpose-built for fast, low-cost stablecoin payments.

Because spending is dollar-denominated, there is no FX guesswork: you are paying with stablecoins wherever Visa is accepted across 180+ countries.

## Fees, rewards and tiers

Plasma One launched a three-tier structure. Lite is free and pays 2% cashback. Core pays 3% plus 5% on qualifying AI spending and bundles ChatGPT Go — normally around US$120 a year, but it has been offered free for the first year with an access code. Platinum pays 4% base, 10% on AI spending and 10% on flights, bundles Claude Pro and ChatGPT Plus, and requires locking 100,000 XPL for twelve months.

Cashback is paid in XPL rather than a stablecoin, so its real value moves with the token. Caps apply per tier — roughly US$250 a month on Lite and Core, and US$1,000 on Platinum.

## Yield and risk

Idle balances can earn up to about 6% variable yield through third-party DeFi vaults. This is the most important caveat: the yield is not a bank deposit, is not insured, and depends on protocol performance. Smart-contract, liquidity, counterparty and market risks mean you can lose part of your principal. Treat the headline APY as variable and risky, not guaranteed.

## Availability and access

Plasma One is invite-only during its current phase. You need a free access code to create an account — official codes are always free, so never pay for one. The card is issued by Rain, a Visa Principal Member, with account services from Bridge, and is available in 180+ countries subject to regional limits. KYC requires an email, phone number and a government ID.
