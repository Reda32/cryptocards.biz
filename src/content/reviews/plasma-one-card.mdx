## Who is Plasma One for?

Plasma One suits people who hold USDT and want to spend it like money while earning cashback. The free Lite tier already pays 2% back, which is generous for a no-fee plan. Heavy spenders, especially on AI subscriptions or flights, can earn more on the paid tiers.

Two things should give you pause. Cashback is paid in XPL, Plasma's own token, so its dollar value moves with the market. And the best tier, Platinum, requires locking 100,000 XPL for a year with no early unlock. If you want stable, predictable rewards, compare it with cards that pay cashback in USD.

## How the card works

You open a Plasma One account in the app, verify your identity and fund it with USDT. You get an instant virtual Visa card, can order a physical card where available, and can add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay depending on your region. The card is issued by Rain, a Visa Principal Member, and works wherever Visa is accepted in 180+ countries.

Account services are provided by Bridge: Bridge Building Inc. for US residents and Bridge Building Sp. for EEA residents. Plasma also offers yield on stablecoin balances through its on-chain ecosystem. Treat that as an investment: it carries DeFi risk, the rate is not guaranteed, and balances are not deposit-insured.

## Tiers and fees

- **Lite (free):** 2% cashback, one virtual card, and a 1% FX markup on non-USD spending.
- **Core (US$199/year, or lock 20,000 XPL for 12 months):** 3% cashback, 5% on AI spending (on up to US$500 a month), up to two virtual cards, a ChatGPT Go subscription and a 0.5% FX markup.
- **Platinum (lock 100,000 XPL for 12 months):** 4% cashback, 10% on AI spending, up to US$600 a year back on eligible flights, up to three virtual cards, and Plasma covers the Visa FX fee. Platinum cannot be bought with cash, and Plasma says the required lock amount can change.

Plasma says it charges no additional fees of its own, but partner fees apply to some transactions such as FX and bank withdrawals, and the app shows them before you confirm. Plasma does not publish ATM fees or spending limits, and ATM withdrawals do not earn cashback.

On Core, the US$199 fee pays for itself through the extra 1% base cashback once you spend about US$20,000 a year, or sooner if you use the AI-spend boost. Remember that the cashback is in XPL.

## KYC and availability

Identity verification is required. You must be 18 or older (or the local age of majority), live where Plasma One is offered, and not be subject to US, UK or EU sanctions; each person can hold one card account. Plasma does not publish a full list of supported countries. Its terms name providers for US and EEA residents, which is what our countries page lists, so check eligibility in the app if you live elsewhere.

## Verdict

Plasma One's free Lite tier is a good stablecoin card for everyday spending, with 2% cashback and Apple Pay and Google Pay support. The paid tiers make sense mainly for high spenders who are comfortable being paid in XPL and, for Platinum, locking a large amount of it for a year. Mind the 1% FX markup on Lite if you spend in other currencies.
