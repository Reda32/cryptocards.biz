## Who the Winity Life Card is for

Winity suits people who hold USDT or USDC and want a cheap way to spend it through Visa. The Exclusive virtual card is free, stablecoin deposits cost nothing, there is no card spend fee, and the card works with Google Pay. If your goal is everyday spending from a stablecoin balance, it is a low-cost card to try.

It is a weaker fit if you hold other crypto, because non-stable assets such as ETH and SOL cost 5% to convert. It also needs a US$25 minimum balance, uses Apple Pay only if Winity adds it later, and publishes neither spending limits nor a country list.

## How the card works

You create an account in the Winity Life app, complete KYC and apply for the card. You then deposit digital assets, which Winity converts directly to a USD balance inside the platform; no crypto is ever loaded onto the card itself. You spend that balance through a Visa card wherever Visa is accepted, which Winity puts at 180+ countries and 150M+ merchant locations.

Winity is run by WTY Technology Hong Kong Limited. It describes itself as a technology provider and programme manager, not a bank, custodian or financial institution: the cards are issued in Hong Kong by licensed partners, which Winity does not name.

Supported assets are USDT and USDC (no fee), plus WCO and non-stable assets such as ETH and SOL (5% conversion). Supported networks are Ethereum, Solana, Tron, Polygon, BNB Chain and W Chain; check the deposit screen for which network each asset uses.

## Fees in practice

- **Stablecoin deposits:** free for USDT and USDC.
- **Other assets:** 5% conversion, so swapping to USDT or USDC elsewhere first is usually cheaper.
- **Card spending:** US$0.
- **ATM:** US$3 per withdrawal, and US$5 to change your PIN at an ATM. ATM operators may add their own fees.
- **Chargeback:** US$100.
- **Physical card:** listed at US$20 a year, with free issuance under a limited promotion. Winity's pages are not fully clear on this, so confirm the price in the app.

The balance rules matter as much as the fees. You need at least US$25 on your balance to complete card transactions; below that, card use is temporarily disabled. A new virtual card is also deactivated if your account does not receive a US$25 deposit within 14 days, unless it is linked to an active physical card.

## Limits and disclosure

Winity does not publish daily, monthly or ATM limits, and it does not publish a list of supported or restricted countries. Its site only says the card is available in 180+ countries, that availability varies by jurisdiction, and that residents of prohibited or sanctioned jurisdictions cannot apply. Check both your eligibility and your limits in the app before funding a large balance.

## KYC and eligibility

You must be 18 or older, complete KYC verification and not live in a prohibited or sanctioned jurisdiction. Winity does not publish the exact documents it asks for; the app shows them during verification.

## Rewards

Eligible purchases earn 1 Winity Point per US$10, which Winity says can be redeemed for travel, dining and other rewards. The Executive tier promises a faster earn rate and a premium metal card alongside the virtual one, but Winity does not publish its price or exact earn rate. Treat points as a small extra rather than a reason to choose the card.

## Verdict

The Winity Life Card is a sensible low-cost option for spending stablecoins: a free virtual card, free USDT/USDC deposits, no spend fee, cheap ATM withdrawals and Google Pay. The trade-offs are the 5% fee on other assets, the US$25 minimum balance, unnamed issuing partners and limited public disclosure on limits and countries. Start with the free virtual card and a small balance, and confirm everything in the app before you rely on it.
