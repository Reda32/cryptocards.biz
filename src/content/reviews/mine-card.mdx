## Important: Mine is pre-launch

Mine is not live yet: sign-up is currently listed as "coming soon". Everything below describes the specifications Mine and independent trackers have announced, and those terms can change before launch. Treat this review as a preview and re-check the details when the card opens.

## Who the Mine card is for

Mine is aimed at freelancers, digital nomads and crypto-curious savers who want a real bank-style account without handing custody of their money to a bank. You get a CHF and EUR IBAN in your own name that settles incoming payments as USDC in a wallet only you control, plus a Mastercard for spending. It is built for people who get paid across borders and dislike the idea that a provider can freeze their balance.

It is a poor fit if you want a card today, if you are a US person (the account and IBAN are not available to you, though the card is accepted in the US), or if you want cashback without paying for a tier. The free Basic card pays no cashback.

## How it works

Money arriving at your IBAN is converted and held as USDC issued by Circle, inside a self-custodial wallet. Spending draws from that balance through a Mastercard issued by Unblock Atomo SARL, an EEA-licensed e-money institution, and accepted in 175-plus countries. Apple Pay and Google Pay work from day one. Mine itself never takes custody, so in principle it cannot freeze what it does not hold.

That custody model is the whole pitch, and it is also the thing to understand clearly: you are responsible for your keys and seed phrase. Lose both your seed and your encrypted backup and nobody, including Mine, can recover the funds.

## Fees and tiers

There are two live tiers. Basic is a US$5 one-off virtual card, free with an invite code, with no subscription and no cashback. Premium is US$124 one-off (US$99 with a code), adds a physical card and 0.5% USDC cashback capped at around US$25 a month, and drops card FX from 1.5% to roughly 0.25%. Card top-ups are 0%, the first 500 CHF or EUR convert to USDC free with no spread, and international ATM withdrawals cost US$5 each. Two further tiers, Metal and Private, have been announced without pricing.

The Basic FX rate of 1.5% is high compared with the best cards, so if you spend often in non-CHF/EUR currencies the Premium tier is where the maths starts to work.

## Availability and risk

The card is broad, but the account is not: Mine is not available to US persons or Russian citizens, or to residents of a small restricted list, and onboarding follows AML rules with extra checks in some countries. The 5% Earn feature is opt-in and routes funds into Aave V3, a non-custodial DeFi protocol: the risk there sits in the smart contract, not with Mine, and the yield is variable and not guaranteed. As with any pre-launch product, the main risks today are execution, unannounced pricing on the upper tiers, and the fact that none of it is battle-tested yet.
