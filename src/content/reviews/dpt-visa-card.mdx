## Who the DPT card is for

DPT suits people who already hold stablecoins and want to turn them into everyday spending without a conversion step. If your balance sits in USDT or USDC and you want a Visa card in Apple Pay or Google Pay within minutes of passing verification, the Oxygen tier does that job with no monthly fee and a small one-off cost.

It is a poorer fit if you are a US citizen, if you live in one of the prohibited jurisdictions, or if you hold most of your crypto as BTC or ETH. DPT is stablecoin-only, so anything outside USDT and USDC has to be swapped elsewhere first.

## How the card works

You download the DPT app, register, and complete identity verification. Once approved, the virtual card is issued immediately and the physical card follows after a shipping step. You fund the account by depositing USDT or USDC over a supported network, and spending settles in US dollars at any Visa-accepting merchant.

The default setup is intentionally open: there is no balance cap and no fixed daily spending limit. Instead, DPT lets you set your own daily and per-transaction caps in the app, which is a genuinely useful control if you are worried about a lost card.

## Fees and limits in practice

The headline numbers are competitive. USD transactions are free, there is no monthly fee on the Oxygen card, and deposits carry no fee. The costs appear at the edges: a foreign-currency purchase attracts an FX markup of up to 2%, ATM withdrawals cost 2% of the amount, and declined transactions cost US$0.50 each after the first three free in a calendar month. Small purchases of US$25 or less currently skip the US$0.10 small-transaction fee while it is waived.

The real constraint is cash. ATM withdrawals are capped at US$250 per transaction and three attempts per rolling 24-hour period, for a US$750 daily ceiling, and declined attempts count toward that limit. On the spending side, unlimited is unlimited, including a single large purchase, so long as you have set your in-app cap high enough.

## KYC and compliance

DPT requires a government-issued ID and a live selfie before issuing a card, which places it in the standard "ID required" bracket rather than the lighter email-only cards. The bigger gating factor is geography: cards cannot be issued to residents of a long list of prohibited jurisdictions, and US citizens are excluded entirely even though non-US citizens resident in the US may apply. DPT operates through DPT (HK) Limited under a Hong Kong TCSP licence, with cards and custody provided by licensed partners.

## Oxygen vs Obsidian

The Oxygen card is the practical product: Visa Platinum, a US$10 virtual or US$50 physical card, no monthly fee, and an automatic upgrade path to Obsidian for qualifying top spenders. Obsidian is a different proposition entirely: a Visa Infinite tier pitched at US$10,000 per year with concierge benefits and travel cover measured in the millions. For almost everyone comparing crypto cards on fees and limits, Oxygen is the tier that matters.

## Verdict

DPT is a credible stablecoin spending card with unusually generous default limits and a clean fee structure for ordinary use. The trade-offs are deliberate: no cashback, a narrow two-coin deposit list, modest ATM ceilings, and country restrictions that rule out some of the largest markets. If you hold USDT or USDC, are eligible in your jurisdiction, and mainly want card spending rather than cash withdrawals, it competes well against other stablecoin-first cards. Confirm your eligibility and the current fees in the DPT app before you commit, since both the fee schedule and the country list can change.
