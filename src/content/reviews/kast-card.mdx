## Who is the KAST card for?

KAST suits people who hold stablecoins and want to spend them like a normal bank card, with cashback. The free Standard plan already pays 1.5% back in USD, there is no fee on top-ups or on USD card spending, and the card works with Apple Pay and Google Pay. It is a strong everyday card if you mostly spend in US dollars.

It is less attractive if you spend mainly in other currencies, because the FX fee runs from 0.5% to 1.75%, or if you rely on cash: ATM withdrawals are expensive and tightly capped. You also need a government ID: KAST is not a no-KYC card.

## How the card works

You fund a KAST account with stablecoins (USDT, USDC, PYUSD or RLUSD) on a supported network, or send other crypto such as SOL, BTC, ETH or XRP, which KAST converts to stablecoins automatically. You then spend through a Visa card, online, in stores, or through Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

Check the network carefully when depositing. KAST lists different networks per coin (for example, USDT on Solana, Ethereum, TRON and others, and USDC on Solana, Ethereum and Arbitrum), and sending on the wrong network can lose funds.

KAST does not set a fixed maximum on card spending, although security, compliance and card-network checks still apply. Transfers of US$50,000 or more need to be arranged with KAST's Concierge team first.

## Plans and fees

KAST is organised as memberships rather than separate cards:

- **Standard (free):** 1.5% USD cashback, two virtual cards included (US$2 for each extra one, and a US$2 issuance fee in some regions), and a physical card with no card fee but US$40 delivery.
- **Premium (US$1,000/year):** 2% cashback plus 1% in KAST Points, physical card included.
- **Private (US$10,000/year):** 3% cashback plus 2% in KAST Points, physical card included.

Paid plans are charged upfront for 12 months; there is no monthly option. All plans share the same transaction fees:

- **Top-ups, USD card spend, Apple Pay/Google Pay:** 0%.
- **FX on non-USD spending:** 0.5% to 1.75%, depending on country and currency. The app shows the exact fee before you confirm.
- **ATM:** US$3 + 2% per withdrawal (plus FX if non-USD), up to US$250 per withdrawal and three withdrawals or US$750 per 24 hours.
- **Declined for insufficient balance:** US$0.50.

The maths on paid plans is simple. Premium's extra 0.5% cashback over Standard only covers its US$1,000 fee if you spend around US$200,000 a year (ignoring the value of KAST Points). For most people, Standard is the right plan.

## KYC and availability

Identity verification with a government-issued ID is mandatory before you can transact. KAST says it serves 170+ countries but does not publish the list: the identity check in the app shows the supported countries, and if yours is missing, KAST is not available to you. Dual citizens may pass with an ID from a supported country. Using a VPN to get around a restriction breaks KAST's terms, and KAST checks location by more than IP address.

## Verdict

KAST's free Standard plan is one of the better ways to spend stablecoins: 1.5% USD cashback, no top-up or USD spend fees, and Apple Pay and Google Pay support. Watch the FX fee if you spend in other currencies, avoid it for cash withdrawals, and only consider Premium or Private if your spending is very high.
