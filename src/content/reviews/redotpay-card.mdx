## Who is the RedotPay card for?

RedotPay suits crypto holders who want to turn stablecoins into everyday spending without moving money through a bank. If most of your balance is USDT or USDC and you travel or shop online across currencies, the low FX fee and free virtual card make it an easy first card to try.

It is a poor fit if you live in the United States, need cashback, or are unwilling to hand over an identity document. There is no rewards programme, and the useful limits only unlock after verification.

## How the card works

You top up the card with crypto from a supported network, the balance is converted to US dollars, and you spend it through a Visa card. The virtual card is issued inside the app and can be added to Apple Pay or Google Pay immediately, so you can pay in stores with your phone before a physical card arrives.

Because settlement happens in USD, the only cost on a typical purchase is the FX fee when the merchant charges in another currency. Top-ups with crypto are free, which keeps the running cost predictable.

## Fees and limits in practice

The headline numbers are simple: no monthly fee, a one-off issuance fee, a 1.2% FX fee and a 2% ATM fee. Where people get caught out is the ATM fee minimum and the decline fee, which applies when a transaction fails after authorisation. Both are small, but they add up if you rely on cash.

Limits start modest and rise once KYC is complete. Plan on verifying early if you intend to use the card as your main spending account.

## KYC and compliance

RedotPay asks for an email and phone number up front, then a government ID and a selfie to issue a card. That places it in the "ID" tier: real names and documents are checked, but proof of address is not always required. The trade-off is fewer surprises at the merchant, since a verified card is less likely to be declined.
