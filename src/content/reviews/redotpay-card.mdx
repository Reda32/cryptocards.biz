## Who is the RedotPay card for?

RedotPay suits crypto holders who want to spend stablecoins and major coins without moving money through a bank. If most of your balance is USDT or USDC and you shop online or travel, the instant US$10 virtual card is an easy way to start, and there is no monthly fee to keep it.

It is a poor fit if you live in a restricted country. RedotPay excludes the United States, mainland China, Russia, India, Indonesia, Singapore, Turkey and around 40 others, so check the countries list before you pay for a card. It is also not the card to pick for cashback: rewards only come with the paid Pro membership.

## How the card works

You deposit crypto into the RedotPay app (USDT, USDC, BTC and ETH are the core assets, with others such as BNB, SOL, TRX, XRP and SUI also supported), then spend through a Visa card. The virtual card is issued in the app once you pay the US$10 fee from your crypto balance. The physical card costs US$100 and is the one you need for ATM withdrawals.

Limits are in your hands: in the app under **Card > Limit** you set a per-transaction and a daily limit for each card. RedotPay does not publish a fixed monthly spending cap. After a security change, such as a new phone number, email or password, spending is temporarily capped at US$1,000 per transaction and per day for 24 hours.

Google Pay works. Apple Pay is more complicated: since 22 August 2025, new cards cannot be added to Apple Pay because of a change to Apple's Hong Kong programme. Cards added before that date keep working, but if you remove one, it cannot be added back.

## Fees in practice

- **Card fee:** US$10 virtual, US$100 physical, one-off and non-refundable. No monthly or annual fee.
- **FX:** 1.2% when the merchant charges in a currency other than your card's currency.
- **Crypto conversion:** 1% when your crypto is converted to pay. On a foreign-currency purchase funded with crypto, the two fees add up to about 2.2%.
- **ATM:** 2% on USD-card withdrawals up to US$10,000 a month, 3% on the amount above that. The ATM operator may add its own fee.
- **Select merchants:** some merchant categories carry a 1.5% fee (minimum US$0.50), with the first three eligible transactions per card each month waived.

**RedotPay Pro** is an optional paid membership: US$12.90 a month or US$129 a year. Monthly members get the virtual card fee waived and 3% cashback on Apple Pay and Google Pay purchases, capped at US$18 a month. Annual members also get the physical card fee waived and RedotPay's ATM fees waived on up to US$1,000 a month. Cashback is paid in USD, can only be spent on card transactions and expires after 30 days. Pro only pays off if you spend enough through mobile wallets or withdraw cash regularly.

## KYC and compliance

Identity verification is mandatory. It runs inside the app through Sumsub, and you cannot use the card without it. Citizens of restricted countries cannot complete verification at all, and restricted locations cannot be used as a billing address. The card also stops working in sanctioned territories, even for verified users travelling there.

## Verdict

RedotPay is a straightforward, low-commitment way to spend crypto through Visa: a cheap virtual card, no monthly fee and limits you control. The costs that matter are the 1.2% FX fee, the 1% conversion fee and the 2% ATM fee, so it is best for spending in the card's own currency. Check the restricted-country list first, and only pay for Pro if you will actually use the cashback or ATM waiver.
