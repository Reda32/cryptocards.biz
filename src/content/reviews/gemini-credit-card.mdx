## Who is the Gemini Credit Card for?

The Gemini Credit Card is for US-based crypto believers who already spend on a credit card and would rather earn Bitcoin than airline miles or cash. If you pay your balance in full each month, the multipliers (up to 4% on gas and EV charging, 3% on dining, 2% on groceries) turn routine spending into a steady passive accumulation into 60+ coins.

It is a poor fit outside the United States, for anyone who does not want a credit check on their file, or for people who need a dependable support line. The card is issued by WebBank and is only available to US residents.

## How the card works

It is a regular metal Mastercard at the point of sale. Rewards accrue on every purchase and are paid instantly in the crypto you choose, held in your Gemini exchange account. Because it is a credit card rather than a prepaid or debit card, you spend on credit and repay from a bank account.

The trade-off is custodial: rewards sit on Gemini until you withdraw them, and moving them to self-custody means network fees. For heavy spenders, tracking hundreds of micro-reward transactions for tax is also a real administrative burden.

## Fees, rewards and limits

There is no annual fee, no foreign transaction fee and no issuance fee, which makes the card cheap to hold. The costs are indirect: a variable APR of roughly 17-29% if you revolve a balance, and a convenience spread on the crypto you withdraw.

The headline 4% rate is category-capped at US$300 of monthly spend, after which it reverts to the base 1%. Treat the high rates as a bonus on specific categories, not an uncapped return.

## KYC, availability and support

Approval involves a hard credit check as well as full identity verification, and community reports consistently flag low credit limits even for strong applicants. The card is US-only, so it is not an option for the EEA, UK or most of the world.

Support is the most common complaint: users describe long email queues, account freezes that lock rewards, and a jurisdictional gap between WebBank and Gemini during disputes. If you value responsive support alongside rewards, weigh that carefully before applying.
